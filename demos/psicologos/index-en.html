A Scientific and Human Approach

I don't believe in generic, one-size-fits-all solutions. I combine empirically validated clinical tools to offer you support tailored to your life story.

  • Cognitive-Behavioral Therapy (CBT)

    We identify maladaptive thought patterns to modify behaviors and emotional responses.

  • Acceptance and Commitment Therapy (ACT)

    We develop psychological flexibility to accept unchangeable situations and align your actions with your core values.

  • Mindfulness & Somatic Approach

    Mind-body integration through mindful breathing techniques to calm the physical symptoms of distress.
